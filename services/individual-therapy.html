Individual Therapy

Individual therapy, at your pace.

One-on-one counseling for adults in Winter Park and Orlando, and by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida.

At a glance

  • Who it's for: Adults, with a special focus on women navigating anxiety, relationships, and heavy seasons
  • Where: In person at our Winter Park office on Aloma Avenue, near Orlando, or by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida
  • With: Cristina Ally, LMHC, NCC, trained in EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART)
  • Fees: $185 per 50-minute session, self-pay. Superbills are available for out-of-network reimbursement, and HSA and FSA cards are welcome.
  • First step: a short phone consultation, about 10 minutes, to see if it feels like a good fit. You can request one through the client portal.

Are you having a hard time handling something in your life right now? Could you use an expert opinion on what you are going through, or a non-judgmental ear that just listens? Would you get the most out of having someone's full attention?

If any of that landed, individual therapy may be what you have been looking for.

What individual therapy is

Individual therapy differs from group and other formats in one simple way: you are the sole focus of a single therapist who helps you define and reach your own goals. The hour is yours. The pace is yours. The direction is yours.

That matters more than it sounds like it should. If you want a say in how fast this goes, in which methods are used, in how much feedback you get, and in when your sessions happen, individual work is where that control lives.

This may be the right fit if

  • You keep circling the same problem, over and over, and cannot get out from under it
  • You do not know who you could trust with the thing you are actually struggling with
  • You wish someone could help you understand why you feel the way you do
  • You are interested in therapy but do not want to hand over control of the process
  • Other parts of your life are being pulled down by this one thing
  • You need someone to listen to you, and to your feelings, for once
  • You are interested in therapy but cannot picture how it would fit into your life

None of those require a diagnosis, a crisis, or a good enough reason.

What adults bring to individual work

Clients come to Flourish for anxiety, depression, self-esteem, relationships and boundaries, post-traumatic stress, and life transitions: the changes that are technically nobody's fault and still knock a person sideways. A move. A breakup. A new role. A career that stopped fitting. A version of the future that quietly stopped being available.

Where trauma is part of the picture, individual therapy is also where trauma recovery work happens, including EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy. And when words are not the easiest way in, sand tray can be used inside an individual session.

Questions people ask before they start

Does going to therapy mean I am weak?

Not at all. People who ask for help know when they need it and have the ability to reach out. You already have strengths that have carried you before, and for whatever reason they are not working right now. Part of this work is identifying what those strengths are and putting them back to use.

Why not just talk to a friend?

A good friend is a real thing, and not the same thing. A mental health professional can help you approach your situation in a new way, teach you skills, offer a perspective you do not have, listen without judgment or expectation, and help you listen to yourself. Therapy is also confidential, so you are not quietly weighing who else might find out.

Why not just take medication?

Medication treats symptoms, and for many people it is genuinely useful alongside therapy. What it cannot do on its own is get to the root. This work is designed to do that: to go into the patterns underneath and build strategies that serve your goals.

How long will this take?

There is no honest general answer, and anyone who offers you one is guessing. It depends on your goals, on your commitment, and on what brought you here. What can be said is that the pace is set with you rather than for you, and that you will know what the plan is at every stage.

Your participation is what makes this work. We see each other for an hour a week. It is the work you do outside that hour that produces the change you came for.

Why self-pay

Flourish is not on any insurance panels, and that is a deliberate choice rather than an oversight. Working outside the panels means you choose your own therapist instead of picking from a network list. It means no mental health diagnosis is submitted to an insurer or added to your record. It means confidentiality stays intact, with no insurer requesting your diagnosis, treatment plan, or progress notes. And it means the length of your treatment is decided by you and your therapist rather than by a managed-care policy.

A superbill can be provided on request, which you may submit to your insurer for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Current fees and the full reasoning live on rates & insurance.

Start when you are ready

Reaching out is the part that feels biggest and takes the least time. You can request an appointment through the client portal whenever you are ready, and we will help you find the right fit.

Questions

Common questions about individual therapy

What can individual therapy help with?

Anxiety, depression, self-esteem, relationships, life transitions, and the seasons that feel too heavy to carry alone. Every person comes in with different goals, so the work is shaped around you.

Is individual therapy at Flourish focused on women?

Much of Cristina's work is with women, and the practice was built around women's healing. You'll find a warm, unhurried space where you don't have to explain the basics first.

Can I do therapy online?

Yes. Sessions are available in person in Winter Park or by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida, whichever feels most comfortable.

Do you take insurance?

Sessions are self-pay: $185 for 50 minutes. If you have out-of-network benefits, we can give you a superbill to submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement, and HSA and FSA cards are welcome.

How do I get started?

Begin with a short phone consultation, about 10 minutes, so you can ask questions and get a feel for whether this is the right fit. You can request one anytime through the client portal.
