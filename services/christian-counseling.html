Services/Christian Counseling

Christian Counseling

Your faith is welcome in the room.

Faith-integrated counseling for adults in Winter Park and Orlando, and by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida. Brought in at your invitation, never assumed.

At a glance

  • Who it's for: Adults who would like their faith to be part of their counseling
  • Where: In person at our Winter Park office near Orlando, or by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida
  • With: Cristina Ally, LMHC, NCC, trained in EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART)
  • Fees: $185 per 50-minute session, self-pay. A superbill is available on request, and HSA and FSA cards are welcome.
  • First step: A 10-minute phone consultation, which you can request through the client portal.

Some people want a therapist who understands that their faith is the frame they see their whole life through. If that's you, you shouldn't have to explain it, defend it, or leave it at the door.

Therapy first

This is licensed clinical mental health counseling, with the same training, methods, and accountability as every other kind of work at Flourish. I'm a Licensed Mental Health Counselor trained in EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy, and the clinical work stays the same when faith is part of the conversation.

What changes is that you don't have to explain why prayer matters to you, or why a passage has been sitting heavily with you all week, or why leaving your church felt like losing a family. Those can simply be part of what we're working with.

How faith shows up in a session

Usually less formally than people expect. It might look like:

  • Talking about what you believe without having to give background first, or brace for a reaction
  • Naming scripture, prayer, or your church community as part of your life
  • Drawing on your faith as a source of steadiness during hard clinical work, when it is one
  • Making room for the parts of your faith that feel strained right now
  • Holding the difference between what you believe and what a person or an institution did to you

Sessions won't open with prayer unless you want that, and I won't hand you scripture as an answer to a clinical problem.

You decide how much faith is part of our work, and you can change it. Some clients want faith woven through everything, some want it acknowledged and otherwise left alone, and some want it nowhere near the room. You can change your mind any time, without needing to explain.

What to know before you come

This isn't pastoral counseling. Pastoral counseling is spiritual care offered through a church, with different training and different accountability. It can be really valuable, and plenty of people have both. I'm the clinical side of that, and I'm not here to replace your pastor.

Faith is never a requirement. Most of the work at Flourish isn't faith-integrated at all, nobody is asked what they believe, and if this page isn't for you, nothing else about working with me changes. I also won't assess how you're doing spiritually, and you'll never be told you'd feel better with more faith.

If your faith feels complicated right now

A lot of people come in because their faith feels shaky right now: doubt after a loss, anger at God that feels unsayable, distance from a community that used to be home. Some carry harm that happened inside a church, from someone who held spiritual authority, which hurts in its own particular way because the place that was supposed to be safe is where it happened.

None of that has to be resolved before you come in, and none of it will be hurried. I won't try to talk you back into certainty on anyone's schedule but yours.

Can EMDR and faith-integrated work go together?

Yes, and they often do. EMDR is a structured clinical protocol, and it doesn't ask you to set your faith aside. When faith is part of what steadies you, we can draw on it deliberately during the work.

Who this tends to be for

Adults who want a licensed trauma therapist and would also like their faith understood. Often that's people who have tried therapy before and spent part of it explaining their beliefs instead of working. Sometimes it's people who have only ever talked to someone at their church and have reached something that needs clinical care as well.

Start when you're ready

You don't have to decide ahead of time how much of this you want. You can bring it up in a first session, or later, or never. If you'd like to talk it through first, request an appointment and tell me what you're looking for.

Questions

Common questions about christian counseling

Do I have to be a Christian to see Cristina?

No. Faith is welcome in the room when you want it there, and never required. You choose how much faith, if any, is part of our sessions.

Will faith come up if I don't bring it up?

Only if you'd like it to. You decide, and you can change your mind any time.

Do you take insurance?

We're not on insurance panels, so all sessions are self-pay: $185 for 50 minutes. I can provide a superbill on request if you'd like to seek out-of-network reimbursement, and HSA and FSA cards are welcome.

How do I get started?

Request a 10-minute phone consultation through the client portal. On that call you're welcome to tell me how much you'd like faith to be part of things, or to leave it for later.
