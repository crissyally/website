Services/Christian Counseling

Christian Counseling

Your faith is welcome in the room.

Faith-integrated counseling for adults in Winter Park and Orlando, and by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida. Brought in at your invitation, never assumed.

Some people want a therapist who understands that their faith is not a hobby or a coping strategy, but the frame they see their whole life through. If that is you, you should not have to translate it, defend it, or leave it at the door.

Therapy first

This is licensed clinical mental health counseling. The training, the methods and the accountability are the same as every other kind of work at Flourish. Cristina Ally is a Licensed Mental Health Counselor trained in EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy, and the clinical work does not change because faith is part of the conversation.

What changes is that you do not have to explain why prayer matters to you, or why a passage has been sitting heavily with you all week, or why leaving your church felt like losing a family. Those can simply be part of what we are working with.

How faith actually shows up in a session

Less formally than people often expect. In practice it tends to look like:

  • Talking about what you believe without having to give background first, or brace for a reaction
  • Naming scripture, prayer, or your church community as part of your life, because they are
  • Drawing on your faith as a genuine source of steadiness during hard clinical work, when it is one
  • Making room for the parts of your faith that are strained right now, rather than steering around them
  • Holding the difference between what you believe and what a person or an institution did to you

It does not mean sessions open with prayer unless you want that, and it does not mean you will be given scripture as an answer to a clinical problem.

You set the dial, and you can move it. Some clients want faith woven through everything. Some want it acknowledged and otherwise left alone. Some want it nowhere near the room. All three are normal, none requires an explanation, and you can change your mind at any point without it being a conversation.

What this is not

It is not pastoral counseling. Pastoral counseling is spiritual care offered through a church, with different training and different accountability. It can be genuinely valuable, and plenty of people have both. This is the clinical one, and it does not replace your pastor any more than your pastor replaces a therapist.

It is not a requirement. Most of the work at Flourish is not faith-integrated at all. Nobody is asked what they believe. If this page is not for you, nothing else about working here changes.

It is not a test. You will not be assessed on how you are doing spiritually, and there is no version of this where you are told you would feel better with more faith.

If your faith feels complicated right now

A lot of people arrive here not because their faith is steady, but because it is not. Doubt after a loss. Anger at God that feels unsayable out loud. Distance from a community that used to be home. Or harm that happened inside a church, from someone who held spiritual authority, which is its own particular kind of injury because the place that was supposed to be safe is the place it happened.

None of that has to be resolved before you come in, and none of it will be hurried. You will not be talked back into certainty on a schedule that suits anyone but you.

Can EMDR and faith-integrated work go together?

Yes, and they often do. EMDR is a structured clinical protocol rather than a belief system, and it does not ask you to set anything aside. When faith is part of what steadies you, it can be used deliberately as a resource inside the work rather than something that sits outside it.

Who this tends to be for

Adults who want a licensed trauma therapist and who also want their faith understood rather than managed. Often people who have tried therapy before and spent some of it explaining their beliefs instead of working. Sometimes people who have only ever talked to someone at their church and have reached something that needs clinical care as well.

Start when you are ready

You do not have to decide in advance how much of this you want. You can raise it in a first session, or later, or never. If you would like to talk it through first, request an appointment and say what you are looking for.
