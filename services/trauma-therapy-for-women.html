Trauma Therapy for Women

Trauma therapy for women, at your pace.

A soft place to do hard work, in Winter Park and Orlando, and by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida.

At a glance

  • Who it's for: Women healing from trauma, abuse, and the heavy seasons that follow
  • Where: In person at our Winter Park office on Aloma Avenue, near Orlando, or by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida
  • With: Cristina Ally, LMHC, NCC, trained in EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART)
  • Fees: $185 per 50-minute session, self-pay. Superbills are available for out-of-network reimbursement, and HSA and FSA cards are welcome.
  • First step: a short phone consultation, about 10 minutes, to see if it feels like a good fit. You can request one through the client portal.

Maybe you've been carrying something for a long time. Maybe you've already talked it through, with a friend, in a journal, or late at night with an AI chat, and you understand it better than ever. And still, it hasn't let go. That is often the moment women find their way to Flourish.

Who this is for

Flourish was built around women's healing. You might be here because of:

  • Sexual abuse or assault, whether recent or long ago
  • Childhood or relational trauma that still shapes how you feel about yourself
  • A relationship that left you doubting your own instincts
  • Anxiety, panic, or sleep that never settled after something happened
  • Grief, or a season that simply feels too heavy
  • Looking fine on the outside while quietly holding it all together

You don't need a diagnosis, and you don't need to have it all figured out. You only need to want something to change.

Healing doesn't require reliving it

You don't have to retell your story over and over to be free of it. The work tends to unfold in three movements:

  • Safety first. We build trust and grounding. You set the pace, and nothing happens before you're ready.
  • Resolve, don't relive. Using EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART), guided techniques help the brain reprocess painful memories so they lose their grip on the present.
  • Grow forward. With the past quieter, we build the skills and self-understanding that carry you into steadier, fuller days.

If you've been talking to AI about it

If you've been working through things with ChatGPT or another AI tool, you're not doing it wrong. Putting feelings into words is real work, and it can help you notice patterns. Therapy adds what a chat can't: a trained person who notices what goes unsaid, keeps you safe while hard memories are processed, and helps the change actually hold. You're welcome to bring what you've written. If it helps you, AI journaling between sessions can become part of the work, too.

About Cristina

Cristina Ally is a Licensed Mental Health Counselor and National Certified Counselor, and the founder of Flourish Counseling Co. She is trained in EMDR, ART, and the Reconsolidation of Traumatic Memories. Her work has included serving survivors of human trafficking and leading recovery groups for women across the Orlando area. Questions

Common questions about trauma therapy for women

Do you offer trauma therapy for women in Winter Park?

Yes. Cristina Ally, LMHC, offers trauma-focused therapy for women in person at our Winter Park office near Orlando, and by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida.

I've been talking to ChatGPT about what happened. Can I bring that to therapy?

Yes, and you're welcome to. Writing things out can be a real first step. Bring what feels useful, and we'll build on it together.

Do I have to tell you everything at the first session?

No. You share what you're ready to share. The early work is about feeling safe and steady, and you set the pace from there.

Which approaches do you use?

Cristina is trained in EMDR and Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART), approaches that help painful memories finally feel like the past, along with expressive work like sand tray.

Do you take insurance?

Sessions are self-pay: $185 for 50 minutes. If you have out-of-network benefits, we can give you a superbill to submit to your insurer for possible reimbursement, and HSA and FSA cards are welcome.

How do I get started?

Begin with a short phone consultation, about 10 minutes, so you can ask questions and get a feel for whether this is the right fit. You can request one anytime through the client portal.
