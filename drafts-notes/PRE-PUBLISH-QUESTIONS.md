licensing, or business questions rather than website ones.

gets changed. Nothing here needs to be stretched to make a better page.

2. It is **in person only** at the Winter Park office, because it needs a physical tray and

   page will rank less well for the query it exists to serve, which is a legitimate
   trade to make deliberately.
3. **The page states she is a Christian herself.** Confirm, and say whether she wants
   any denominational detail named. The draft names none.
4. **The section on church hurt and spiritual abuse.** The page has a passage on doubt,
   anger at God, distance from a community, and harm done by someone holding spiritual

5. **Does this change the answer to A3** (whether the groups page mentions the Christian
   curriculum)? The two decisions are related and are cleaner decided together.
6. **Should `/about` gain one sentence** acknowledging she can integrate faith when a
   client wants it? Recommended, because it gives the entity signal without touching
   her positioning. One sentence, not a section.

**Not blocking, do it anyway:** her Psychology Today and TherapyDen profiles both have
faith and denominational filters. If those boxes are unchecked, checking them may do
more this month than the page will, and it costs no deploy. If they are already checked,
that explains the queries.
