Services

Care shaped to where you are.

Every person walks in with a different story. These are the ways we can work together, in person in Winter Park or by secure telehealth anywhere in Florida.

Adults

Individual Therapy

One-on-one counseling for anxiety, depression, self-esteem, relationships, and life transitions, at your pace and on your terms.

Specialty

Trauma Recovery

Specialized care to gently resolve traumatic memories so they lose their grip on the present, without endlessly reliving the story.

Trauma

EMDR

A proven, structured therapy that uses guided bilateral techniques to help the brain reprocess distressing memories so they no longer feel raw.

Expressive

Sand Tray

A gentle, hands-on form of expressive therapy that uses a tray and miniatures to process what can be hard to put into words. No experience needed.

Community

Group & Recovery Groups

Guided groups where shared experience becomes shared strength, including closed recovery groups for women.

Seasons

Grief Counseling

Compassionate support through bereavement and loss, and the complicated seasons of grief that don't follow a tidy timeline.

All sessions are self-pay. See rates & insurance for fees, or read our FAQ for common questions.

In Person or Online

Wherever you feel most comfortable.

Every service above is offered in person at our Winter Park office, or by secure, private telehealth, so you can do this work from wherever you feel most at ease.

Online sessions are available to clients anywhere in Florida.
