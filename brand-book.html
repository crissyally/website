Brand book & build reference

Everything you need to rebuild Flourish, in one place.

This is the design spec behind the reference site: the exact colors, fonts, logo usage, page structure, and photo direction. Use it side-by-side with the live example to recreate the site in Squarespace. Every decision here is already made, so you're copying, not choosing.

02 — Typography

One elegant serif, one clean sans.

Two typefaces do all the work. Fraunces (a warm serif) is the editorial voice for titles and quotes. HK Nova (a clean sans) handles everything else: body text, headings, labels, and buttons. The logo wordmark uses a third font, Bayshore, but that only ever appears inside the logo image, so you never set it yourself.

Fraunces
The serif voice. Used for: page titles, section headings, and italic pull-quotes. Free in Squarespace's built-in Google font list, just search "Fraunces."
HK Nova
The body sans. Used for: paragraphs, subheadings, eyebrow labels, buttons, navigation. A licensed font (needs Business plan to upload). Free fallback: Hanken Grotesk or Figtree, both very close.

03 — Logo usage

Two versions, picked by background.

Use the dark logo on light backgrounds (cream, blush, white) and the white logo on dark backgrounds (espresso, black). Keep clear space around it, around the height of the "F", and don't recolor, stretch, or add effects. In the header it sits at about 42px tall.

04 — Buttons

Pill-shaped, two jobs.

All buttons are fully rounded pills with uppercase HK Nova labels. The solid espresso button is the primary action (usually "New Clients"); the outline button is secondary ("Existing Clients" / "Learn more"). On the dark call-to-action bands, buttons flip to blush and cream.

05 — Section looks

Four backgrounds, alternating down the page.

Every section uses one of four "looks." Alternating them is what gives the site its calm rhythm. In Squarespace these map to section color themes.

Cream
Default. Espresso text, blush-deep eyebrows, dark buttons.
Blush
Accent sections. Espresso text/eyebrows for contrast, dark buttons.
Dark band
Espresso background, cream text, blush eyebrows. The call-to-action.
Footer
Black background, taupe text, cream headings.
06 — Site architecture

Eight pages, one shared shell.

Top nav is Home · About · Services · Our Team · Rates · Resources. Contact lives in the footer only. Every page shares the same header, footer, and dark call-to-action band, so you build those once and reuse them.

07 — Photography

Calm, warm, lived-in.

Soft natural light, cozy interiors, mugs and blankets and quiet windows, never clinical or stocky. Photos are gently warmed to sit in the blush/cream family. People photos are candid and relaxed.
