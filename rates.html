What is a superbill?

It’s an itemized receipt you may submit to your insurance company for possible out-of-network reimbursement. It includes service and diagnosis codes, and reimbursement depends on your individual plan.

Why Self-Pay

The benefits of private pay.

We're currently not on any insurance panels. Many clients find a self-pay approach gives them more freedom and privacy in their care.

Choose your own therapist

You're not limited to a panel. You choose the fit that's right for you.

No diagnosis on your record

Your mental health information is never submitted to insurers or the Medical Information Bureau.

Full confidentiality

No insurer requesting your diagnosis, treatment plan, or progress notes.

No session limits

You and your therapist decide what's right, not a managed-care policy.

Payment

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, and HSA & FSA cards. Payment is handled securely through the client portal.

Cancellation policy

If you're unable to attend, please cancel at least 24 hours in advance. Otherwise, you may be charged the full session fee.

Good Faith Estimate

Under the No Surprises Act, you have the right to a Good Faith Estimate of expected costs. We're happy to provide one on request.
